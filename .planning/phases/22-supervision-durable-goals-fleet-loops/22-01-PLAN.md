<objective>
Establish the two foundations every other Phase 22 plan consumes and none of them may re-derive: a measured, cross-binary verdict on whether the existing F12 session journal admits Goal/Task/Wait records, and the one durable Goal/Run kernel that owns objective, completion contract, authority snapshot, budget snapshot, evidence, cursor, wait and terminal state.

Purpose: Success Criterion 5 requires journal compatibility to be PROVED or MIGRATED EXPLICITLY without silently invalidating accepted F12 behavior, and the advisory that governs this phase says that determination happens BEFORE the kernel is built, not after. Success Criterion 3 requires five different execution engines to terminate through ONE canonical Goal transition, which is only achievable if the terminal taxonomy exists before the first adapter is written. Both of those are this plan. Getting either wrong propagates into 22-02, 22-03 and 22-04 and cannot be corrected there.
Output: A recorded compatibility verdict backed by a real journal written by one binary and resumed by another on both Linux and Windows; the Goal vocabulary in `wcore-types` so the protocol crate can name it without depending on the agent crate; the durable kernel appending to the existing chain; the single terminal taxonomy including a verified state a model judge cannot reach; and a stored pre-change journal corpus that keeps F12 honest forever.
</objective>
