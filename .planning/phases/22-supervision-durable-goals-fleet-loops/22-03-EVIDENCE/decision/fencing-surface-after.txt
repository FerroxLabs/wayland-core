FENCE-PATH: workspace reservation accounting cite=crates/wcore-swarm/src/worktree_manager.rs:235
  WAS review-only. STILL REVIEW-ONLY. NOT CLOSED.
  Why it was not closed, stated as a reason rather than an excuse: TaskAuthority
  must be unforgeable, which means it must live in the crate that constructs it
  (wcore-agent). wcore-swarm sits BELOW wcore-agent in the dependency graph
  (wcore-agent depends on wcore-swarm; wcore-swarm depends on wcore-config,
  wcore-sandbox, wcore-types, wcore-memory). So this accounting path cannot
  require a &TaskAuthority without an upward dependency the crate map forbids.
  Moving the type down to wcore-types would not help: a constructor public enough
  for wcore-agent to call is public enough for anything else to call, and the
  unforgeability is the whole mechanism.
  Closing it properly needs one of: (a) lifting the admission decision up into
  the ledger so the accounting read is downstream of a fenced claim, or (b)
  passing a capability object down through the swarm API. Both change a seam this
  plan's scope boundary puts out of bounds.
