CROSS_PROCESS=Yes, there is a clear TOCTOU (Time of Check to Time of Use) hazard here. Because `dispatch_gate` is only a per-process `tokio::Mutex`, two concurrent `swarm dispatch` calls in separate OS processes can simultaneously read a retained worker count of N-1, both evaluate the limit as unsatisfied, and both allocate worktrees, resulting in N+1 workspaces. However, this is a concurrent capacity-enforcement bug, not a split-brain task fencing violation, so an epoch-based `TaskAuthority` check would not prevent it regardless.
