WHY THE TWO REVIEW-ONLY ONES MATTER MOST:
The heartbeat/status write and the workspace reservation accounting are both
reached from inside a worker's own checkout, by a process that already holds the
directory. Neither currently passes through a value that a fencing token could
be attached to by construction, so making them refuse a superseded owner would
depend on a contributor remembering. That is precisely the "silent hole" the
lease-with-fencing-token option's own con names.

A NOTE THE PANEL SHOULD WEIGH: the durable Goal transition path added in this
lane (crates/wcore-agent/src/goal/kernel.rs) is already structurally fenced by
construction - SessionJournal::append refuses every Goal variant, so the kernel
is the only writer. That is an existence proof that structural_guard=possible is
achievable on this codebase rather than aspirational, and it is the pattern any
task-claim fencing would follow.
