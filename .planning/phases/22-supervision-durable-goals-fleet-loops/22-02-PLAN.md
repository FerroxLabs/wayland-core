<objective>
Make Direct, ForgeFlows, Fleet, Council and Anvil terminate through exactly one canonical Goal transition, with exactly one loop owner and no nested verification or retry owner — after first measuring, against the source, what each of those five engines actually produces today.

Purpose: This is Success Criterion 3 and it is the hard one. Five engines already exist, each with its own terminal vocabulary, its own retry bound and its own verification story. Writing five adapters from a brief would produce five subtly different notions of "done" wearing one name, and the failure would only surface when a Goal resumed into a state no strategy could explain. The census comes first for the same reason Phase 21's did: its brief was wrong on its central claim, and inheriting it would have propagated the error into everything downstream.
Output: A per-strategy census measured at execution time; a strategy adapter surface on which terminating outside the canonical transition is structurally impossible; an explicit loop-owner claim with a refusal for nesting; Anvil's gate evidence consumed directly so a verified state stays host-observed; and a live exercise that drives all five engines through the real shipped binary and proves each one left exactly one canonical terminal transition in the journal it wrote.
</objective>
