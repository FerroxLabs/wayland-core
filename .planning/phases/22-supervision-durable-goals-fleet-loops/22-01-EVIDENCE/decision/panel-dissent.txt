## The losing option: explicit-versioned-migration

STRONGEST ARGUMENT ANY MEMBER MADE FOR IT (internal adversarial pass, and echoed
in weaker form by codex): the version number is what tells a reader what content
model to expect. Under `additive-at-current-version` two journals both stamped
v5 can differ materially — one written before Goal records existed, one after —
and anything that needs to distinguish them must scan the content of an
append-only chain rather than read a header. Secondarily: M3 showed the
pre-change binary reporting a version-skewed journal as `corrupt complete frame
15`. Under `explicit-versioned-migration` the same user would get
`UnsupportedSchema { found: 6, supported: 5 }` — a diagnosis instead of an
accusation. And the migration probe measured the option's price at only 3 of 76
tests, so the cheap option's main advantage over it had just evaporated.

WHY THAT ARGUMENT DID NOT CARRY. The option's own `cons` paragraph states the
condition: "this is the expensive option and it is only correct if Task 1 showed
the additive path is not transparent". Task 1 measured the opposite with a
byte-identical SHA-256 over the reduced state (M1) and a prefix-identical
reduction after an append (M2). The header-cannot-distinguish argument is real
but argues for a content-level capability marker, not for burning a version
number and turning 32 occurrences across 4 files plus every existing user's
journal into migration subjects. The corrupt-versus-unsupported argument is a
DIAGNOSTICS defect, and the proportionate response is to improve the error
message, not to bump the schema — it is filed to BACKLOG as a MEDIUM below. And
a bump introduces a new risk the option's own cons names: the 49-entry
`LEGACY_EVENT_TYPES` list would have to be re-frozen at a new boundary, and
getting that wrong "silently admits a new event into an old frame".

## The losing option: escalate

STRONGEST ARGUMENT ANY MEMBER MADE FOR IT (internal adversarial pass): the live
corpus contains no `tool_execution_*` frame because the provisioned key returns
401, and the tool region is the densest, most deeply nested part of the reduced
state; and the Windows leg was not in the bundle at panel time while the writer
lease is `#[cfg(unix)]`-gated with a recorded prior defect class about mandatory
byte-range locks. A determination that names two platforms and measures one is
strictly a one-platform determination.

WHY THAT ARGUMENT DID NOT CARRY. The `escalate` option is defined as "the records
cannot be carried without invalidating accepted F12 behavior". M1 and M2 falsified
"cannot be carried" directly. Voting escalate on a measured negative is not
caution, it is refusing to read the instrument. Kimi added the sharpest form of
the counter: the COMPATIBLE-AT-V5 question is a SCHEMA question about serde
decode, exhaustive reduction and the version-gated boundary check, all of which
are platform-independent Rust compiled from one source; the Windows gap belongs
to a different claim (writer-lease correctness across restart) whose outcome
cannot logically flip the version decision.

## Arguments made AGAINST the option that was committed to

- (adversarial, codex) An older binary reading a newer journal fails closed with
  a message that says "corrupt". Accepted as a real user-facing defect and filed
  to BACKLOG rather than dismissed.
- (adversarial) `SESSION_JOURNAL_SCHEMA_VERSION` stops implying a content model.
  Accepted; the mitigation is an explicit content-level marker, recorded as a
  constraint on Task 3 rather than as a reason to choose differently.
- (adversarial, kimi) The corpus has no tool frames. Accepted as a named
  limitation of the determination. Task 3's Test 6 stores this exact corpus as a
  permanent regression fixture, and the limitation is carried into the SUMMARY
  rather than being closed silently.

## VERDICT_SOUND=no objections

There were none: all four members answered `VERDICT_SOUND=yes`. Two of the four
(the internal adversarial pass and kimi) qualified their yes, and those
qualifications are preserved here because a qualified yes that gets recorded as a
bare yes is how a limitation disappears:

- The evidence supports the verdict as a SCHEMA verdict. It does not by itself
  support the full two-platform durability story the plan's verdict language
  implies.
- M5 (writer lease across restart) was measured on Linux only. Windows is a
  separate, still-open observation.
- Task 3 must not close F22-06 on Linux-only evidence.
