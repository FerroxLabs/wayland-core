## WHAT THE MEASUREMENT MEANS FOR THE OPTION

3 of 76 is a small, bounded, well-understood repair set: all three are
version-boundary tests that assert the exact constants. It is NOT free, and the
32 occurrences across 4 files are real edit surface, but the expensive option is
cheaper than its `cons` paragraph implies. The probe therefore does NOT
disqualify `explicit-versioned-migration` on cost; the decision has to turn on
whether the migration is NEEDED, which is Task 1's question, not this one's.
