The `additive-at-current-version` option should be authorized. The fundamental purpose of a schema migration is to safely bridge a structural discontinuity between how data was written and how it must be read. The empirical evidence from Task 1 demonstrates that no such discontinuity exists for this change. A clean fail-closed on downgrade is standard and acceptable for minor additive updates, making the additive approach the most responsible and least risky path.
