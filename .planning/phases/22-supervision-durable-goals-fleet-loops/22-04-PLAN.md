<objective>
Give Goal, child, task, wait, log, cursor and terminal state one typed Core command and event surface that the CLI, the TUI and the host protocol all adapt without reinterpreting; make session-local loops bounded and unable to outlive their session; and emit the canonical serialized producer fixtures the later D2 consumer gate replays.

Purpose: This closes Success Criterion 1 and Success Criterion 4. Criterion 1 explicitly names three paths observing and controlling IDENTICAL state, which no test suite can establish — three surfaces that each pass their own tests can still disagree, and the only proof is driving one real Goal and reading it back from all three at once. Criterion 4's loop bounds are equally unprovable from a fake clock: a machine that sleeps and wakes is the thing that breaks interval scheduling, and the only way to observe it is to suspend a real process and count what happens on resume.
Output: One typed command and event set with three thin adapters; a bounded loop policy with an enforced non-claim against durable triggers; the D2 producer corpus regenerated under one minor bump; and live exercises that drive the real binary and prove the three surfaces agree, that a suspended-then-resumed loop does not burst, and that a reconnect after a gap resumes at the right cursor.
</objective>
