DECISION, RATIONALE AND DISSENT — 28-03 task 1, "what is an unacceptable quality/performance
delta?"

DECISION: OPTION C — drift within the run as the primary gate, plus deliberately loose
absolute sanity floors. UNANIMOUS, 4 of 4.

  codex  (gpt-5.6-sol)      C, high
  gemini (3.1-pro-preview)  C, high
  kimi   (K3)               C, medium
  internal adversarial      C, medium

RATIONALE (the panel's shared reasoning, not a restatement of the option text)

A alone is blind to the failure mode this candidate is most plausibly in: a run that is
uniformly slow or uniformly wrong from session 1 produces a drift of ZERO and passes. Three
members reached that independently and it is the decisive fact. B alone invents per-platform
ceilings with no per-platform baseline and dresses guesses as empiricism; codex added the
sharpest form of it — "using the same limits on every platform is more honest than fabricating
platform-specific baselines" — which is why the floors below are identical on all three
families rather than tuned per box. D is circular by construction: a threshold derived from the
run it judges cannot fail that run, so it would make Criterion 2 unfalsifiable for exactly the
candidate it is meant to certify.

C is therefore not a compromise between A and B. It is the only construction in which BOTH
failure modes — degradation over the run, and a run that was bad throughout — have a mechanism
that can fire.

DISSENT AND MINORITY POSITIONS — recorded because they are the useful part

1. THE STRONGEST OBJECTION, raised by the internal pass and independently confirmed by codex
   when probed separately: IN THIS SOAK THE LATENCY-DRIFT GATE IS STRUCTURALLY NARROW. Every
   session is a fresh short-lived process. A per-process leak cannot accumulate across 1,000
   separate processes — it dies with each one. Latency drift can therefore only fire through
   state that OUTLIVES a process: accumulated on-disk state that later sessions re-read,
   orphaned children competing for CPU, or host contention that is not the product's at all.
   Codex, asked the question in isolation and with no knowledge of the panel: "It cannot detect
   process-local leaks — those die with each CLI process — or uniformly bad latency present
   from session 1."
   CONSEQUENCE, adopted into the design rather than noted and ignored: the detection weight for
   "no unbounded resource use" sits on the SLOPE bands (state-dir bytes, live product
   processes, harness handles), NOT on the drift bands. The results document may not present a
   green latency drift as though it were the finding of a leak. It is the absence of one narrow
   symptom.

2. GEMINI'S EARLY WINDOW — sessions 101-300, discarding 1-100 as cold start — WAS OVERRULED by
   the internal pass. The direction is safe (a faster baseline makes the ratio stricter) but it
   deletes cold-start pathology, which is a real product property, from the measurement.
   ADOPTED INSTEAD: blocks 1-3 including block 1, with the per-block series published so a
   reader sees the cold start rather than having it removed for them.

3. GEMINI AND CODEX BOTH CHOSE A SINGLE-WINDOW p95. OVERRULED in favour of kimi's block
   aggregation: compute the statistic per 100-session block, then compare the MEDIAN OF THE
   THREE EARLY BLOCKS against the MEDIAN OF THE THREE LATE BLOCKS. A p95 over one 200-session
   window has about ten samples above it on a host whose load average is 3-5 and not ours; one
   foreign cron job in the late window manufactures a breach, and gemini's own key objection
   concedes exactly that. Block aggregation is not a loosening — it is the same threshold on a
   less noisy estimator, and a spike must now move the median of three separate blocks.

4. KIMI'S "LOAD-ADJUSTED INCONCLUSIVE" VERDICT WAS REJECTED, and this is the one place the
   panel's own instincts were overruled on program grounds rather than technical ones. A third
   verdict that is neither pass nor fail is precisely the shape an agent on this program abused
   when it invented a termination state to escape an artifact it did not want to produce. A
   regression adjustment on a covariate the certifier also chooses is a dismissal lever with a
   statistician's vocabulary. ADOPTED INSTEAD, and strictly weaker for the certifier: record
   the load covariate as evidence; it may justify exactly one re-run on a quiet host; a breach
   is RED until a re-run is green ON ITS OWN NUMBERS; there is no verdict in between. Gemini
   and codex independently proposed the same shape, so the minority here is kimi alone.

5. CODEX'S WARM-UP OBJECTION IS THE ONE THAT CHANGED THE DESIGN MOST. "Warm-up may bind
   variable values, but it may not define 'whatever happened' as correct. Otherwise a uniformly
   broken response could teach the validator that broken output is normal." This is the same
   defect class as the whole phase's positive-control rule, one level up: an invariant learned
   from a broken baseline is an invariant that cannot fail. ADOPTED: warm-up must satisfy a
   COMMITTED sanity schema (exit 0, non-empty output, no panic sentinel) before it establishes
   any invariant; a surface failing it is BROKEN INVENTORY and establishes nothing; and if
   broken inventory exceeds 5% of the workload the run is VOID rather than passed.

6. KIMI'S STRUCTURAL DIGEST was adopted in its affordable half — the warm-up captures a
   structural signature (top-level JSON key set, or line count plus sentinel absence) as well
   as exit class, because "exits 0 but silently malformed" is the most plausible quality-rot
   mode under resource pressure and it is free to catch.

7. KIMI'S SUPERSEDES CLAUSE was adopted verbatim in intent. Every number in bands.json is
   invented; none has a measurement behind it. The bands file says so in a machine-readable
   field (`numbers_are_measured: false`) which the validator ENFORCES, and it carries the
   requirement that a later phase re-derive the bands from this soak's archived distributions
   for the NEXT candidate. Doing that for THIS candidate would be option D.

WHAT REMAINS UNRESOLVED, stated rather than smoothed over

The proxy being banded is a DETERMINISM-AND-STABILITY measurement, not a semantic quality
measurement. Kimi said it plainest — "exited 0 and produced non-empty output would pass a
surface emitting garbage" — and gemini agreed in different words. A soak can still pass while
the product is semantically wrong on all 1,000 sessions, provided it is wrong CONSISTENTLY and
was already wrong at warm-up. The warm-up sanity schema and the broken-inventory VOID reduce
this; they do not cure it. Semantic correctness belongs to the per-surface probe matrix
(plan 28-02), not to the soak, and the soak's results document must say so in those words
rather than let "quality" carry an unearned meaning.

The four thresholds — 1.5x on the p50 block median, 2.0x on the p90 block median, 0.02 absolute
on the correctness rate, and the floors at 0.99 / 10s p95 / 60s max — are PRE-REGISTERED
GUESSES. Their defensibility comes from three things and no others: they were committed before
any number existed, they are labelled as unmeasured in the artifact itself, and widening one
after a measurement is named as a finding against the certifier rather than a correction to the
band.
