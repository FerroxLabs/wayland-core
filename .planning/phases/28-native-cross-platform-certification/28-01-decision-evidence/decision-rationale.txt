DECISION: c4-disposition, with the three internal amendments attached as executable rules.

Phase 28 acceptance gate, decided 2026-07-27 at planning time, before any plan was written,
because the answer determines the shape of the whole plan set rather than one task inside it.

PANEL SPLIT: 4 of 4 for c4-disposition. This is the MAJORITY position, so no
MINORITY-POSITION-ADOPTED clause applies, and the panel was not tied so no EVIDENTIARY-TIEBREAK
applies. The internal adversarial pass concurred CONDITIONALLY and its conditions are adopted
in full; they are not optional colour on a unanimous vote, they are part of the decision.

=== THE CONFLICT ===

ROADMAP Phase 28 Success Criterion 4: "Zero findings remain at every severity before
acceptance." REQUIREMENTS F28-04: "No critical case is skipped and every finding at every
severity is resolved before certification acceptance."

Against AGENTS.md's standing policy: "CRITICAL/HIGH must be fixed, or disproved with executable
evidence. MEDIUM and below go to BACKLOG, non-blocking. Do not invent a stricter rule — that is
what turned Phase 20 into a 74-plan loop lasting two weeks."

=== THE RULE ADOPTED ===

"Resolved" is implemented as DISPOSITIONED, not as FIXED. The acceptance gate is:

  ZERO findings at any severity lack an explicit, recorded, evidence-backed disposition.

Four terminal dispositions exist, and every finding takes exactly one:

  FIXED     — repaired, with the repair proved by an executable check.
  DISPROVED — shown not to be a defect, with executable counter-evidence.
  ACCEPTED  — real, not repaired, carried knowingly. Requires a named rationale, a named owner
              and a BACKLOG id. Available at MEDIUM and LOW ONLY.
  DEFERRED  — real, routed to a named later phase. Same three requirements. MEDIUM and LOW ONLY.

CRITICAL and HIGH have exactly two available dispositions: FIXED or DISPROVED. Neither ACCEPTED
nor DEFERRED is reachable at those severities, and the gate must fail closed rather than warn.

Every ACCEPTED and DEFERRED finding is enumerated INSIDE the signed certification receipt, with
its severity, rationale, owner and BACKLOG id, so it is visible at acceptance and again at
release rather than absorbed into a backlog nobody re-reads.

=== THE THREE BINDING AMENDMENTS (from the internal adversarial pass) ===

A1. SEVERITY IS RE-SCORED AGAINST PHASE 28'S OWN FOUR SUCCESS CRITERIA. No finding enters the
    ledger at a severity inherited from an earlier phase. The inherited severity is recorded as
    provenance only. Rationale: the dangerous findings on this candidate do not need anyone to
    downgrade them — they ARRIVE below HIGH, scored against a different phase's criteria.
    Inherited severity is a laundering channel that requires no launderer.

A2. A FINDING THAT CONTRADICTS A PHASE 28 SUCCESS CRITERION IS CRITICAL OR HIGH BY CONSTRUCTION
    AND CANNOT TAKE THE ACCEPT OR DEFER PATH. Not by judgement — by construction, because the
    criterion it contradicts is the thing being certified. Two named consequences, both of which
    this program is currently carrying as sub-HIGH and both of which are hereby re-scored:
      - `live_future_drop_reaps_descendant_job_tree` is a descendant-process-tree reap failure.
        Criterion 2 says the soak completes with "no orphan process." Same subject. It is HIGH
        for Phase 28 and cannot be accepted.
      - The Windows AppContainer ACL lease SID/profile mismatch, under which the product can run
        with the sandbox SILENTLY DISABLED, is a security control that reports itself active
        while inactive. Criterion 1 is the hostile platform matrix including sandbox probes.
        Same subject. It is CRITICAL for Phase 28 and cannot be accepted. Criterion 1 must force
        it into the open rather than inherit it.
    A2 is what stops c4-disposition from collapsing into c4-standing-with-paperwork.

A3. THE RECEIPT MAY NOT CLAIM WHAT IT DOES NOT HAVE. Its defensible assertion is exactly: zero
    undispositioned findings, zero skipped critical cases, zero unresolved CRITICAL/HIGH
    findings. It may NOT assert "zero known defects" or "zero findings", and the receipt
    verifier must REJECT a receipt that asserts either. A certification that overstates itself
    is worse than one that reports a gap, because Phase 29 and the release consume it.

=== WHY, ON THE EVIDENCE RATHER THAN THE COUNT ===

1. The criterion's own requirement text says what it is protecting. F28-04 pairs "every finding
   … resolved" with "no critical case is skipped." Those two clauses are coverage and
   accounting. The protected property is that the ledger is COMPLETE and every entry has a
   closed fate — not that every fate is "fixed." A program that fixed nothing but silently
   deleted its MEDIUM findings would satisfy a literal empty-ledger gate while destroying
   exactly what the criterion protects.

2. The program has RUN both rules and measured both. The literal rule (block on LOW,
   security_block_on=low) produced Phase 20: 74 plans, roughly two weeks. The amended rule
   produced Phase 20A: three Success Criteria closed in 4 plans. Repealing the one measured
   process improvement, on the phase with the largest matrix and the greatest finding surface,
   is the worst possible place to repeal it.

3. THE DECIDING FACT, and it is executable rather than arguable. The internal pass raised the
   only argument that would have defeated the consensus: if Criterion 4 was authored while the
   standing severity policy already existed, then it is a deliberate, informed, one-time
   elevation of the bar and an agent must not read it back down. That claim was checked:

     ROADMAP Criterion 4 text      -> 0192e3c0, 2026-07-19 19:01:55 +0700
     REQUIREMENTS F28-04 text      -> 0192e3c0, 2026-07-19 19:01:55 +0700
     severity-policy amendment     -> d0837aa7, 2026-07-25 12:20:08 +0700

   Criterion 4 PREDATES the amendment by six days. It was written under the old regime — the one
   the program then measured, found catastrophic, and replaced with an amendment whose own text
   says "do not invent a stricter rule." Criterion 4 is therefore a survival of a superseded
   rule, not an informed elevation above the current one. Applying it literally would honor a
   superseded draft over the human's own later, measured correction. The later instrument
   governs. This single check is what carries the decision; the 4-0 vote merely agrees with it.

4. c4-literal is additionally UNREACHABLE as written, which matters because an unreachable gate
   is met by reclassification rather than by fixing. Several open sub-HIGH items are
   architectural: bash cannot run under Windows AppContainer at all, by construction — msys
   needs \BaseNamedObjects and AppContainer confines to AppContainerNamedObjects. No budget
   fixes that. A gate that can only be satisfied by relabelling FORCES the severity-laundering
   attack that c4-disposition merely has to defend against.

=== THE COST ACCEPTED, IN THE OPTION'S OWN TERMS ===

"'Resolved' is being read as 'dispositioned', which is a weaker word than the criterion chose."
Accepted, and it is the honest characterisation. The mitigation is A3: the receipt states the
weaker claim it can actually support, in those words, and a verifier rejects the stronger claim.
The reading is only honest because nothing is erased — every finding stays in the ledger and in
the signed artifact.

"A determined executor can still accept a genuinely serious MEDIUM." Partly accepted, and A2 is
what narrows it: any finding that touches the subject matter of one of the four criteria cannot
reach the accept path at all. What remains acceptable is, by construction, a finding that
contradicts none of the four things this phase certifies. That residual risk is real and is
carried knowingly.

"It adds rubric and cross-audit machinery to the phase." Accepted. The rubric is written once in
28-01 and consumed mechanically by 28-04.

=== OPEN RISK CARRIED FORWARD, NOT CLOSED HERE ===

The receipt is an accounting control, not a technical one. It records that a defect was known;
it does not prevent that defect from reaching a user. This decision therefore makes Phase 29
(supply chain and release integrity) and the release gate the places where an ACCEPTED finding
gets its second look, and 28-04 must hand them a machine-readable list rather than prose. If
Phase 29 does not read that list, the accounting control has no consumer and this decision is
worth less than it looks. That is a real dependency and it is stated rather than assumed.

=== WHAT IS NOT RESERVED TO SEAN ===

This is an evidence-acceptance policy decision. Under the program's standing rule, the only
reserved actions are merging to main, opening a PR, tagging, releasing, closing a GitHub issue,
deleting a retained evidence ref, and supplying real credentials. Evidence-acceptance policy is
explicitly NOT among them, and the measured cost of routing such decisions upward was 18
blocking human gates across 32 plans. The decision is taken here and is final for Phase 28.
