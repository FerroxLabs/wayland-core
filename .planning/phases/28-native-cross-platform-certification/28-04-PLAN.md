<objective>
Bind the certification evidence into a signed receipt whose every claim is independently recomputable from the raw artifacts, adjudicate every finding under the acceptance rule the panel settled, and state Phase 28's verdict against its four Success Criteria with the evidence for each and the gaps named.

Purpose: a signature binds authorship, not truth. The whole value of this plan rests on the verifier recomputing each binding from the retained logs and result artifacts rather than trusting the receipt's own summary, because a self-attesting receipt is this program's most expensive failure class wearing a signature. The acceptance rule is already decided — zero findings lacking a terminal disposition, with FIXED or DISPROVED the only options at CRITICAL and HIGH, and the accept path closed entirely to anything that contradicts one of the four criteria. This plan enforces that mechanically and then grades the phase honestly against what the criteria say.
Output: a phase-scoped signed receipt binding candidate, platform, posture, corpus, environment, artifacts, logs and skip policy, enumerating every accepted and deferred finding; a finding ledger where every entry is re-scored against Phase 28's criteria; and a four-criterion verdict that claims no seal, no trust root and no release.
</objective>

**THE TWO AMENDED PHASE RULES — stated verbatim, and they bound this plan.**

- Findings at CRITICAL or HIGH must be fixed or disproved. MEDIUM and below are logged to BACKLOG and do not block execution.
- Execution begins when no CRITICAL or HIGH finding is open, or after 2 review rounds, whichever comes first. A third round is not permitted; it escalates to Sean.

**NON-NEGOTIABLE, stated verbatim.** A REPORTED RED IS WORTH FAR MORE THAN AN ENGINEERED GREEN — never weaken an assertion, `#[ignore]`, `#[allow]`, re-gate, delete a test, or raise a timeout to reach a gate.

**THE ACCEPTANCE RULE IS SETTLED AND THIS PLAN ENFORCES IT.** Decided by the four-way panel at planning time, 4-0 for disposition-based acceptance with three binding amendments; the full record and its dissent are in `28-01-decision-evidence/`. Read `decision-rationale.txt` and `decision-dissent.txt` FIRST. The gate is: zero findings at any severity lack an explicit, evidence-backed terminal disposition. FIXED and DISPROVED are the only dispositions available at CRITICAL and HIGH. ACCEPTED and DEFERRED each require a named rationale, a named owner and a BACKLOG id, and are available at MEDIUM and LOW only. Do not soften A1, A2 or A3.

**RECOMPUTE, DO NOT COPY.** Every binding in the receipt is derived from the raw evidence by the verifier and compared against what the receipt claims. Reading a count out of a results summary and writing it into the receipt proves the summary was read. Digest the actual logs, count the actual cells, and reject on any disagreement. A receipt that agrees with itself and disagrees with the artifacts is REJECTED, and that rejection is a finding about the run.

**NO SEAL, NO TRUST ROOT, NO RELEASE.** The receipt is evidence, not authorization. Sign with a PHASE-SCOPED key minted for this certification; record its public half and fingerprint. Do NOT bind that key to any release trust root, do not rotate or publish a trust root, and do not describe the receipt as a seal. Reserved to Sean and not performed here: merging to main, opening a pull request, tagging, releasing, closing a GitHub issue, deleting a retained evidence ref, and supplying real credentials. Trust-root and revocation work belongs to Phase 29.

**NEVER PRINT, ECHO OR TRANSMIT A SECRET.** If any step appears to need a real credential, it is out of scope: record it as a blocker in the SUMMARY. Do not embed one and do not copy one from the host.

**THE VERDICT QUOTES THE CRITERIA.** Each of Phase 28's four Success Criteria is quoted VERBATIM from ROADMAP.md and graded MET, MET WITH STATED EXCEPTIONS, or NOT MET. MET WITH STATED EXCEPTIONS is a first-class grade so there is no incentive to narrow a criterion to reach MET. If the phase's goal was not achieved, say so — every phase on this program that graded itself honestly produced more value than one that claimed a green.

**TERMINATION CRITERION FOR THIS PLAN (hard).** This plan extends ONE schema, produces ONE receipt, adjudicates ONE ledger and states ONE verdict. It terminates in exactly one of four states, and in all four it writes its SUMMARY and stops:
1. **Complete — criteria met.** Receipt produced, independently verified by recomputation, every finding dispositioned legally, and all four criteria graded MET with the evidence for each. Write the SUMMARY and stop.
2. **Complete — criteria met with stated exceptions, or not met.** As above, but one or more criteria are limited or NOT MET. The verdict states exactly which criterion, limited by what, and what evidence would close it. This is a PASSING state for the plan and an HONEST one for the phase, and it is the expected outcome if the carried Windows process-tree reap failure reproduced in plan 03.
3. **Terminated — the evidence cannot be bound.** Recomputation disagrees with the artifacts in a way that cannot be reconciled, meaning the upstream evidence is not trustworthy. STOP, do NOT sign a receipt over it, record precisely what disagreed, and escalate. A receipt is worth nothing if it is signed to get past this step.
4. **Escalated — the schema extension cannot be made to work.** Bounded at TWO repair iterations, where an iteration is one edit-build-run cycle on real hardware. After the second, STOP, record every remaining failure with its output, and escalate.
Under no circumstances does this plan repair a production defect, modify any production file under `crates/*/src` outside `wcore-eval-scenarios`, modify or delete an existing test — including `tests/receipt_contract.rs`, which is why the new contract lives in its own file — re-run the matrix or the soak to obtain a better number, claim a seal, bind a trust root, or create a fifth Phase 28 plan.

**THE PHASE PLAN CAP.** Phase 28 is capped at FOUR plans. This is the fourth and last.
