F26-04-A        NEW, this plan, FIXED in-plan. Found by the hostile case

F26-04-B        NEW, this plan. On macOS (APFS) BOTH the case-only and the
                normal-form-only peer name distinctions COLLAPSE at
                materialisation — measured, not assumed, by the generator's own
                post-creation verification on Sean's own Mac. On Windows (NTFS)
                the case-only distinction collapses and the normal-form one does
                NOT. Consequence: an operator migrating on macOS loses one of two
                case-distinct peer profiles at the SOURCE, before the product
                ever sees it, and the product cannot detect what it was never
                shown. MEDIUM, and it belongs to Phase 28's native certification
                because macOS is not a gate host in this phase.

F26-04-C        NEW, this plan. `escape-symlink-dir` (an entire skill directory
                replaced by a symlink out of the root) is not traversed at all,
                so the product emits no refusal NAMING that item; the case's
                evidence is the sentinel digest and the item's absence from the
                store rather than a named refusal. That is a weaker signal than
                the file-level escape cases, which do produce
                `refused: imported executable content contains a symlink`. LOW,
                and recorded so nobody later mistakes the case for stronger
                evidence than it is.
