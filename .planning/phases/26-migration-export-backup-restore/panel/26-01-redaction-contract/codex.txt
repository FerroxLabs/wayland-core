The contract still does not hold strictly. `CredentialRef::name` and `CredentialRef::source_file` are public `String` fields and `CredentialRef` derives `Deserialize`; a hostile document can deserialize a credential value into either field, after which serde, Debug, Display, and errors can emit it. `CredentialRef::new(secret, ".env")` is another direct construction path.

The probe covers genuine discovery and the now-closed `details` paths, but it does not test hostile deserialization or direct construction of `CredentialRef` itself. No supplied evidence shows an observable leak in the measured documents, so this is cosmetic rather than `contract-leaks`.
