`CredentialRef` is structurally safe: it has no value field, variant, constructor parameter, or accessor.

But `DiscoveredItem::details: BTreeMap<String, String>` still permits arbitrary secret-bearing values. `scrub_detail` sanitizes selected write paths; it does not make secrets unrepresentable. Because `details` is public and deserializable, direct insertion, struct construction, or deserialization can bypass `insert_detail` and carry a credential value into serde, `Debug`, or errors. The extended probe validates the known projection paths, not the emitted type’s structural invariant.
