INTERNAL ADVERSARIAL PASS — executor, panel member four
=======================================================

The three external members split three ways: codex real-time-full, kimi
accelerated-except-absolute-deadline, gemini escalate. There is no consensus to
argue against, so this pass argues against the BEST-REASONED of the three
(kimi's), then against gemini's, then steelmans the objection to the option I
end up naming — because a pass that only attacks the options it rejects is a
vote wearing a rebuttal's clothes.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CASE AGAINST kimi's accelerated-except-absolute-deadline
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kimi's argument is the strongest on the table and it turns on one sentence it
did not check:

  "for active-runtime legs you don't need to fake anything; downtime isn't
   charged, so a compressed journey is honest by construction."

That is true about budget arithmetic and false about Success Criterion 5. The
criterion does not say "the active-runtime budget arithmetic is correct across a
restart". It says a multi-day wait, resume and complete journey preserves
cumulative authority, resource, evidence, memory and delivery state with exactly
one loop owner at every resume point. What real days buy is almost entirely
OUTSIDE the budget model, and kimi's licence to compress discards all of it:

  (a) THE WAIT CONDITION BECOMES UNFALSIFIABLE. The journey's wait is satisfied
      by real elapsed time. Compress the span and the wait completes on the
      first resume — which is the exact failure the plan names: "a wait that was
      pending on day one is still pending on day two and completes on the day
      its condition is met, not on the first resume after it." Under a
      compressed span there is no observation that could distinguish a wait that
      correctly stayed pending from one that never could have.

  (b) THE ENVIRONMENT HAS NO CLOCK SEAM EITHER, AND IT IS HALF THE CLAIM. A
      compressed journey never exercises systemd-tmpfiles or tmpreaper reaping
      the state directory, log rotation moving the run log, filesystem cache
      eviction, a real OS reboot, or — the sharpest one — a stale writer-lease
      file whose recorded PID has since been REUSED by an unrelated process.
      PID reuse is a genuine single-loop-owner hazard that only becomes probable
      over days and that nothing in the budget model measures. The probe's
      45-second gap cannot produce any of these, and kimi treats a 45-second
      real gap as having already bought what three days buy.

  (c) OPTION 3'S OWN TEXT IS SELF-DEFEATING GIVEN THE MEASUREMENT KIMI CITES.
      Option 3 reserves real elapsed time for "the absolute-deadline leg, which
      always runs against real elapsed time on every platform". The same probe
      run kimi quotes also emitted
      F23_04_ABSDEADLINE_PRODUCT_CONSTRUCTION_SITES=0 and
      F23_04_ABSDEADLINE_PRODUCT_REACHABLE=false. There is no product-reachable
      absolute-deadline leg to run. Option 3 therefore reduces to "accelerate
      everything, hold nothing in real time" — real-time-full's opposite wearing
      its clothes — and every platform's span assertion becomes trivially
      satisfiable with no compensating real-time leg anywhere in the journey.
      Kimi engaged with the CHARGING line and never with the REACHABILITY line.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE CASE AGAINST gemini's escalate
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Gemini reasons: no seam exists, so acceleration is impossible; real-time-full
needs three platforms; macOS cannot run; therefore nothing is executable;
therefore escalate. The first three steps are correct and the conclusion does
not follow.

It conflates two different questions. The POLICY question is "which legs must
use real time". The REACHABILITY question is "which legs can run at all". A
blocked macOS leg answers the second and says nothing about the first. The plan
supplies the mechanism for exactly this: termination state 2, complete with
named open requirements — the shape Phase 20A closed in, on three met criteria
with four requirements honestly open.

Escalating here would also cost more evidence than it saves: it leaves the two
REACHABLE platforms unexercised as well, at a calendar saving of zero, on a
criterion the plan itself calls the one most likely to reveal a cross-restart
defect. And the plan defines deadlock narrowly — no option drawing three of four
positions AND no minority carrying demonstrably stronger evidence. The second
conjunct fails: the seam measurement is decisive minority evidence. This is a
split, not a deadlock, and escalate is not available for a split.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE STEELMAN AGAINST MY OWN POSITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

real-time-full's literal text says "on all three platforms". macOS cannot run:
the phase's controlling instruction forbids Cargo on the Mac, and
scripts/f23-macos-binary.sh was never landed by 23B-01 or 23B-02, both of which
recorded that conflict and escalated it rather than resolving it. So
real-time-full is not literally executable either, and I must not paper over
that by pretending it is.

The honest reading, and the one I commit to: real-time-full is a rule about HOW
a leg must run — no leg may be accelerated, every span claim must be a real
span. It is not a prediction that every leg WILL run. A platform whose binary is
unobtainable records OPEN for a reachability blocker, with the blocker named,
under termination state 2. That is materially different from recording it green
on a simulated span, which is what both accelerated options would license.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. POSITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The measurement removes two of the four options outright rather than making them
expensive. Thirteen candidate environment seams were attempted and every one was
inert; the shipped binary's --help exposes zero clock, deadline or now flags;
unix_millis() is SystemTime::now() with no override, trait seam or test hook.
F23_04_CLOCK_INJECTION_SEAM=none is not a preference, it is the absence of a
mechanism. Both accelerated options are arguments about a thing that does not
exist. escalate requires a deadlock and this is a split. One option remains, and
it is also the one the criterion's own wording demands.
