<objective>
Turn three declared-but-unexecuted supply-chain properties into measured, bound evidence: generate a byte-deterministic SBOM from the locked dependency graph, make the dependency policy that already exists actually run, and measure reproducibility honestly enough to record either identical digests or a named variance class — then bind all three into a release manifest signed by a throwaway packaging key and prove that mutating any of them breaks verification.

Purpose: F29-01 names five things. Two of them — provenance and artifact signing — the release pipeline already does. Two of them — SBOM and dependency policy — exist as zero and as an unexecuted file respectively, measured at base. The fifth, reproducibility, has never been checked at all. The failure mode this plan is built against is producing three plausible artifacts nobody can independently regenerate, which is exactly what a nondeterministic SBOM and an unmeasured reproducibility claim would be.
Output: a deterministic SBOM generator with an offline determinism contract; the dependency policy executing in a new workflow and a justfile recipe, with its real verdict recorded whatever it is; a two-build reproducibility measurement with both digests and, where they differ, the identified varying input class; and a signed release manifest binding all three whose verification demonstrably fails when any bound digest is mutated.
</objective>
