F29-02-SOURCE-ARTIFACT-IDENTITY — what moved, and what did not
==============================================================
Commit: 3658c4281fe228af1f700a56a42e662d3d6a9c7c

29-01 named this the central finding of the census (F29-CEN-10, HIGH):

  | object                              | digest                          | who vouches |
  |-------------------------------------|---------------------------------|-------------|
  | the ARCHIVE users download          | Sigstore attestation subject    | gh attestation verify |
  | the extracted BINARY that was eval'd| receipt.identity.binary_sha256  | the Phase 28 receipt's CI signature |

and measured that NOTHING in the tree related those two digests to each other.

WHAT THIS PLAN CLOSED — one of the two joins
---------------------------------------------
The signed release manifest carries `artifacts[]` with name, sha256 and
byte_length. Before this plan nothing compared the bytes actually downloaded
against that list, so those digests were decorative: a correctly signed
manifest would have sat beside whatever archive the source handed over.

`VerifiedManifest::check_archive` now binds them, and `self_update::run` calls
it on the downloaded file immediately BEFORE `verify_provenance`, ANDed with it
and never substituting for it:

    manifest.check_archive(&archive_name, &digest, bytes)

Refusals are typed and distinct: `ArtifactNotInManifest` when the manifest does
not name the archive this host needs at all, and `ArtifactDigestMismatch`
carrying expected and actual digest AND byte length. Proved by
`the_downloaded_archive_must_match_the_digest_the_manifest_signed`, which
carries its pristine control (the named digest and length are ACCEPTED) before
each of its three mutations.

So: MANIFEST <-> ARCHIVE is now bound, in the shipped updater.

WHAT THIS PLAN DID NOT CLOSE — the other join, and it is the important one
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MANIFEST <-> CERTIFIED BINARY remains open. The manifest models it as
`certification: Evidence<CertificationBindingV1>` and every manifest this lane
produced carries:

    "certification": { "state": "unavailable",
                       "code": "phase_28_certification_binding_not_yet_available" }

That is deliberate: the binding is 29-01's requirement R28-A on Phase 28, filed
in 29-01-RECEIPT-INTERFACE.md, and Phase 28 is executing concurrently. Phase 29
invents no receipt field. Until R28-A lands, a release manifest still cannot
honestly claim that the certified candidate is the artifact users download —
it can now only claim that the archive users download is the archive the
manifest names.

This clause is therefore graded PARTIAL, not MET. Grading it MET on the archive
half alone would be exactly the downward redefinition this program has already
paid for.
