=== DISPOSITION AT THIS COMMIT ===
F29-02-H1 is SUSTAINED as HIGH, with one of its two legs CORRECTED.

SUSTAINED: .cargo/audit.toml states the parent trace as a 'sole path'
  (quick-xml 0.39.4 <- plist 1.9.0 <- syntect 5.3.0 <- wcore-cli) and grades the
  threat UNREACHABLE. The lockfile at this commit shows a SECOND path onto the
  SAME affected version: wcore-tools 0.12.25 depends on quick-xml 0.39.4
  DIRECTLY, behind the DEFAULT-ON doc-extract feature, and uses it to parse
  docx/pptx OOXML parts from USER-SUPPLIED files. That input is attacker-
  controlled, nothing calls with_checks(false) anywhere in crates/, and both
  RUSTSEC-2026-0194 and -0195 are denial-of-service advisories against exactly
  that parser. The suppression's stated premise is therefore FALSE.

CORRECTED: the earlier framing attributed reachability to calamine's 25
  .attributes() call sites. calamine 0.26.1 resolves to quick-xml 0.31.0, which
  the advisories do not name. That leg does NOT land on the affected version and
  is withdrawn. The finding does not depend on it.

NOT PROVABLE HERE: no proof-of-concept .docx/.pptx was constructed. This is a
  static reachability argument on the dependency graph and the parser's input
  provenance, not an executed exploit. Substitution point: a crafted OOXML part
  driven through wcore-tools' doc_tool against quick-xml 0.39.4.
