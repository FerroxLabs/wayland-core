full-nine-verbs — the losing option, in its own terms.

The contract document recorded nine verbs and the code will now carry seven plus
`run`. A reader who finds the contract before the summary will believe the product
has `gateway doctor` and `gateway logs`. That divergence is real and is only
acceptable because it is written down here and in the summary.

`logs` in particular was dismissed too quickly by the three external members as
"diagnostics, not lifecycle". Journey step 10 asks for confirmation that the
PLATFORM'S OWN service mechanism brought the runtime back after a hard kill. The
only in-product evidence of an unattended relaunch is what the relaunched process
wrote. Without a `logs` verb the journey reads $WAYLAND_HOME/gateway.log directly,
which proves a file exists on disk rather than proving the product can show an
operator that its service restarted it. Phase 24 therefore closes that clause on a
narrower claim than the criterion reads, and the narrowing is recorded rather than
absorbed.

`doctor` has the weaker case of the two: Criterion 1's profile-isolation clause is
already observable through `status --json`'s `profile` field and the pid lock's
home comparison, so `doctor` would be a second projection of facts the status verb
already carries.

separate-binary — also not chosen, and its one genuine merit should be recorded:
it is the only option under which the two shared fenced files (wcore-cli/src/lib.rs
and main.rs) are never opened at all. Five lanes edit those two files concurrently
in this execution and every additive edit is a merge hazard. That merit lost to
three defects, but the hazard it names is real and the edit made here was kept to
one `pub mod` line and two adjacent blocks for exactly that reason.
