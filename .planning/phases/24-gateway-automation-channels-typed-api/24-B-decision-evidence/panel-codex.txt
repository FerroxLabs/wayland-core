## THE QUESTION

What should be built for `crates/wcore-cli/src/gateway.rs`?

OPTION full-nine-verbs
  Build all nine verbs from the contract (install, start, stop, restart, status, doctor,
  logs, drain, uninstall) plus the hidden `run` daemon entry point.
  PRO: matches the recorded contract exactly; nothing left for a later plan.
  CON: doctor and logs are not needed by any Criterion-1 or 24-04 journey step; the
  budget spent on them is budget not spent proving install/start/kill/recover live.

OPTION journey-minimal
  Build `run` (the actual long-lived supervised runtime: acquires the pidlock, opens the
  ledger, starts the AutomationPlane, serves until signalled) plus install, start, stop,
  restart, status, drain, uninstall. Defer `doctor` and `logs` with a named, recorded
  gap, on the grounds that both are diagnostics rather than lifecycle and neither appears
  in any journey step or Success Criterion clause.
  PRO: every verb built is a verb the live journey exercises, so all of it gets proved
  rather than asserted; closes EVIDENCE 3's defect.
  CON: the phase's own contract document listed nine; shipping seven means the contract
  and the code disagree unless the gap is recorded.

OPTION separate-binary
  Do not put the surface in wcore-cli. Ship a separate `wayland-gateway` binary (its own
  [[bin]] or its own crate) so the shared fenced files are never touched.
  PRO: zero contention on the two shared files.
  CON: contradicts the crate's own documented placement; the service units invoke
  `<wayland-core> gateway run`, so this ALSO requires changing service.rs on all three
  families; the operator now has two binaries; and a [[bin]] in wcore-gateway would need
  clap added to that crate, which IS a manifest+lockfile edit the lane is forbidden.

OPTION do-not-create
  The surface belongs elsewhere or is already served; do not create the file.
  If you pick this you must name what then satisfies "native service lifecycle, drain,
  restart, upgrade and rollback work without lost or duplicate delivery" (Criterion 1).

Weigh especially: (a) does EVIDENCE 3 make this a defect fix rather than a feature?
(b) is deferring `doctor` and `logs` honest or is it scope-shaving? (c) given a finite
budget, is it better to build seven verbs and LIVE-PROVE them, or nine verbs and prove
fewer?
