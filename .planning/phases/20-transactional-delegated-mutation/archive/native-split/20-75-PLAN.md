## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| retained parent handle ← handle-relative rename | The destination of every atomic publish is named by a held handle rather than a pathname; this is the entire anti-swap contract, and the primitive enforcing it has never actually executed on Windows |
