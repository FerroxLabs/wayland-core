<objective>
Prove that nested reservation, refund, escalation, approval, cancellation and result delivery stay attributable to the correct parent and session — on the real `wayland-core` binary, not only in process — and then state Phase 21's verdict against its three Success Criteria with the evidence for each.

Purpose: Criterion 1 asks whether a child got more than it should. Criterion 2 asks whose books its activity landed on, and a system can pass the first while failing the second — the child gets no extra authority, but its refund credits the wrong parent, its approval prompt is answered by a different session, or its result is delivered to a sibling. Nothing in the 21-02 corpus tests that, because that corpus asks what the child OBTAINED. Attribution is also the more user-visible half: a human sees which work asked for approval, which work stopped when they cancelled, and which turn a result attached to. That makes the shipped binary the only honest place to prove it.
Output: An attribution corpus with at least two siblings per case, live drivers reading `parent_call_id` off a real `--json-stream` run and attribution off the rendered TUI screen, results measured on Linux and Windows, and a phase verdict that states the evidence for each Success Criterion and the gaps honestly. No seal is claimed.
</objective>
