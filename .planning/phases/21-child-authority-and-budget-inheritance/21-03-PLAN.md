Close the CRITICAL and HIGH findings the corpus measured, at the seams the census named, and nothing else.

Purpose: 21-02 produced an independent, severity-classified measurement of where a child can widen a parent's authority or resource envelope across eleven dimensions and two surfaces. Under the amended rules that measurement fixes this plan's scope exactly: CRITICAL and HIGH are fixed or disproved, MEDIUM and below go to BACKLOG. Both are Sean's decision, not an executor's.
Output: A triage of every red with one disposition each, one blocking authorization of the repair set, the authorized repairs implemented at the named seams within two repair iterations, a re-run of the corpus stated as a delta against the 21-02 measurement, and every MEDIUM-and-below finding logged to BACKLOG as explicitly non-blocking.

**THE TWO AMENDED PHASE RULES — stated verbatim, and they bound this plan.**

- Findings at CRITICAL or HIGH must be fixed or disproved. MEDIUM and below are logged to BACKLOG and do not block execution.
- Execution begins when no CRITICAL or HIGH finding is open, or after 2 review rounds, whichever comes first. A third round is not permitted; it escalates to Sean.

**NON-NEGOTIABLE, stated verbatim.** A REPORTED RED IS WORTH FAR MORE THAN AN ENGINEERED GREEN — never weaken an assertion, `#[ignore]`, `#[allow]`, re-gate, delete a test, or raise a timeout to reach a gate.

**A REPAIR IS NOT CLOSED UNTIL THE REAL PRODUCT REFUSES THE WIDENING.** Success Criterion 1 is a security claim, and the evidence that closes it is the shipped `wayland-core` binary refusing the actual widening at the surface a user touches — not a guard function returning false in a unit test. Every repair in this plan is re-verified by re-running the corpus in ALL FOUR combinations from 21-02: standalone and host-protocol, each in-process and LIVE against the real binary. A repair whose in-process case goes green while its LIVE case stays red is NOT closed; that divergence is the single most important signal this plan can produce, because it means the fix landed somewhere the product does not reach. In-process tests stay required and are never sufficient on their own. Concretely: v0.6.0 of this product shipped `wcore-permissions` as orphan code that compiled and passed its own tests while no consumer called `PolicyEngine::check`, and no in-process suite would have caught it.

Every live re-verification records three things and is not accepted without all three: the EXACT invocation — binary, flags and input; an OBSERVABLE OUTCOME that distinguishes a working restriction from a broken one; and the NAMED PLATFORM. "Verify it works" is not a verification step and no gate in this plan accepts it. The shipped surfaces are `wayland-core --json-stream` for host protocol (driven by `crates/wcore-eval-scenarios/src/runner.rs`), `wayland-core -p "<prompt>"` or `--no-tui` for standalone headless, and the bare binary on a real PTY for the interactive TUI (driven by `crates/wcore-eval-scenarios/src/pty_capture.rs`, which is `#![cfg(unix)]`, so the live TUI runs on Linux and macOS but not on Windows). Every live invocation asserts the MODE it landed in before its verdict is recorded, because `crates/wcore-cli/src/main.rs` falls through from the TUI to the line REPL whenever stdout is not a terminal.

It terminates in exactly one of four states, and in all four it writes its SUMMARY and stops:
1. **Complete.** Every CRITICAL and HIGH finding is fixed or disproved, every MEDIUM and below is in BACKLOG marked non-blocking, the corpus was re-run on both platforms and the delta against the 21-02 measurement is recorded with every residual named. Write the SUMMARY and stop.

## What this plan does NOT change (scope fence)

- **Any finding the corpus did not measure.** The repair set's boundary was drawn by an independent pass, and that independence is the only reason it is trustworthy. A defect noticed in passing gets a severity and a BACKLOG entry, not a patch.
- **Any MEDIUM-or-below finding.** They go to `.planning/BACKLOG.md` marked non-blocking. Repairing one because it looks quick is how a bounded set stops being bounded, and the amended rules give it no other disposition.
- **Any corpus case not covered by an authorized DISPROVE.** Editing a case triaged FIX ships a real defect with a green light on it, and it is the specific forgery the RED-over-GREEN rule names for this plan.
- **The snapshot-restore intersection helpers, unless a finding is actually about the restore path.** `intersect_execution_budget` and `intersect_caps` are not on the child-spawn seam; changing them to satisfy a spawn-path finding would change the wrong mechanism.
- **`limit_for`'s reporting contract.** It exists to populate an error payload. If a consumer wrongly makes a decision from it, the consumer is repaired to ask the ancestor-walking question instead.
- **The census and the corpus results — consumed, never re-derived.** 21-01 owns the census and 21-02 owns the measurement. Re-deriving either here is a second cycle.
- **The admission gate — read, never re-verified.** 21-01 owns that determination.
- **F21-03 attribution — 21-04 owns it.** Reservation, refund, escalation, approval, cancellation and result-delivery attribution is a different property with a different seam set.
- **No new crate and no new dependency** unless an authorized repair genuinely requires an unreachable symbol, raised at the checkpoint rather than added quietly.
- **No fifth Phase 21 plan is created or proposed.**
