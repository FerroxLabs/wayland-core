F25-SC4 — is the Phase 25 Windows orphan claim dependent on the escalated known-RED?

TEST:   wcore-sandbox::live_integrity::live_future_drop_reaps_descendant_job_tree
STATUS: deterministically RED on Windows, ESCALATED (handoff §4). NOT fixed by this plan.

VERDICT: INDEPENDENT.

REASONING (not an assertion):
  * That test exercises the Job Object REAPING path — whether dropping a future
    tears down a descendant tree.
  * The Phase 25 Windows orphan claim is an OBSERVATION, not a reaping claim. The
    scanner enumerates what is present. In the live exercise the reap was performed
    by Stop-Process, not by the sandbox's Job Object teardown.
  * The scanner reports a surviving descendant whether or not the Job Object reaped
    it. That is precisely what it is for, so its correctness does not rest on the
    mechanism the RED test covers.

CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY:
  Phase 25 does NOT claim that the Windows Job Object reaps descendant trees.
  It claims only that the scanner can SEE whether one survived — and that claim is
  backed by a planted-orphan measurement on SeanDesktop (scannerPlanted=1,
  independent Win32_Process enumeration=1).
  A Windows "orphans: 0" from this scanner means "nothing was left behind in this
  run". It does NOT mean "the Job Object mechanism is proven".
