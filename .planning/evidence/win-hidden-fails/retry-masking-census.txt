RETRY-MASKING CENSUS — every `FLAKY` line in two consecutive full CI runs
=========================================================================

Question: is `[profile.ci] retries = 2` turning red into green anywhere other
than the six Windows tests this lane was sent to re-measure?

Answer: YES. Twelve retried-away failures across two runs, on TWO platforms,
and not one of them left a diagnostic behind.

Method: `gh run view -R FerroxLabs/wayland-core --job <id> --log`, then grep for
`FLAKY` and the `Summary [` line. All six job logs pulled in full; counts below
are from those logs, not from any dashboard.

`FLAKY n/3` reads: passed on attempt n of a maximum of 3. So `FLAKY 2/3` is one
failure, `FLAKY 3/3` is TWO consecutive failures. The summary line counts every
one of them in the PASSED total.

THE DIAGNOSTIC IS GONE
----------------------
Counted directly in job 91372229704's log:

    grep -c 'FLAKY'   ->  6
    grep -c 'TRY 3'   -> 66      (final failures, printed)
    grep -c 'TRY 1'   ->  0
    grep -c 'TRY 2'   ->  0

Those six FLAKY lines account for at least eight failed attempts (four of them
from the two `3/3` rows). Not one printed a TRY line, a panic message, or any
captured stdout/stderr. Cause: `[profile.ci] status-level = "fail"` prints only
a test's FINAL status. Fixed in this lane by moving it to `"retry"`; the
`default` ("pass") and `eval` ("all") profiles were already above that level.


THE TWO ROWS THAT ARE NOT FLAKY AT ALL
--------------------------------------
`multi_worker_output_exhaustion_fails_without_retaining_buffers` was FLAKY 3/3
in BOTH runs: four consecutive failed attempts, reported as passed twice.

`wcore-cli::child_authority_corpus corpus_egress` and `corpus_fan_out` were
FLAKY 3/3 on macOS in run A — same shape, different platform, and nobody has
looked at them either. They are NOT in this lane's six and are recorded here so
the next reader does not have to rediscover them.
