F-GROK-L1 — A REAL INSTALL IMPORTS AN EMPTY ROOT PROFILE, SILENTLY.  (open)
  The real config.toml carries [cli], [marketplace] and [ui] and nothing the
  importer reads: no [models] default, no [mcp_servers]. So grok/root lands
  with provider=None and model=None — a profile that cannot be used to talk to
  anything — and warnings is EMPTY.
  Cause: grok.rs's "imported empty" warning is guarded by
      config.model.is_none() && !has_credential
  and the second conjunct is false on any logged-in install, which is the
  normal state. The warning therefore cannot fire on the very case it names.
  NOT FIXED in this lane: changing it is a product behaviour change outside a
  test-and-ledger brief, and it would alter output other tests read. Recorded
  so the next lane decides deliberately rather than rediscovering it.
