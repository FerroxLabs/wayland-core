# The aggregate now grades what it depends on, and WHY

c1 is fixed in 0.13.12 at 9e92f3bb5 and confirmed green in CI -- the STEP, not just the job
label. c2 was the residual Codex named during that cross-review, and is fixed at 47da0ee86:
a `skipped` dependency is now graded on whether its own job definition declares a reason it
may not run, so an accidental skip is no longer indistinguishable from a rationed one.

The two halves are the same defect at two altitudes. c1 was "the aggregate grades a set it
does not derive"; c2 was "the aggregate grades a conclusion it does not interrogate". Both
were closed by making the workflow itself the only source of truth -- `toJSON(needs)` for
the set, `jobs:` for the permission -- rather than by adding a list beside it.

The issue stays `open` here because closing it is a release action, not a lane's.
